# Mapping the Dark Coil

An interactive, spoiler-aware map of Peter Fehervari's Dark Coil.

**[Open the map](https://needlesong.github.io/dark-coil-map/)**


## What's in it

- **The map.** Characters, places, factions, objects and recurring phrases across 19 stories. Zoom in and the entries spread out to make room for their names.
- **Graded connections.** Every link is marked as *confirmed* (stated in the text), *strong* (not stated, but several specific details match), *speculative* or *echo* (a repeated image or phrase), with the stories it comes from.
- **Guided tours.** Seven storylines told in order, stop by stop: the Angels Resplendent, the Koronatus Ring, Phaedra, Sarastus, the scarred dark man, the Calavera and the Inquisition, and more.
- **Timeline.** An in-universe chronology, built on the reading order Fehervari has given in interviews.
- **Documents.** The scriptures, codices, logs and rhymes that exist inside the setting.
- **References.** Allusions to literature, myth, religion, film, art and the wider 40K setting, graded Clear, Likely, Possible or Tenuous.
- **Questions.** What the stories leave open, with the evidence on each side.
- **From the author.** Paraphrased statements from published interviews, with links.

## Spoilers

The map covers everything, endings included. Use the **Spoilers** button to tick the stories you've read; anything drawing on the others is hidden, and can be revealed one item at a time. Your choice is saved in your own browser.

## Coverage

Covered: *Fire Caste*, *Requiem Infernal*, *Cult of the Spiral Dawn*, *Cast a Hungry Shadow*, *The Reverie*, *Fire and Ice*, *Out Caste*, *A Sanctuary of Wyrms*, *Nightfall*, *The Crown of Thorns*, *The Thirteenth Psalm*, *The Sins of My Brothers*, *Vanguard*, *The Greater Evil*, *Altar of Maws*, *The Walker in Fire*, *Aria Arcana*, *Nightbleed* and *Nightshift Nineteen*.

Not yet covered: *Cold Covenant* and *Blindsight*.

## Corrections and suggestions

Every detail and quotation has been checked against the books, but mistakes happen and readings differ. Open an [issue](../../issues) to point out an error, argue a grading, or suggest a connection or reference. Please give the story and, if you can, the passage.
